Windsor Guest Invoice Assistant

A private invoice workflow operated by Martin Flint for Peaceful 2-Bed Bungalow, Workspace & Garden in Bramley, Rotherham, UK.

The assistant is being set up to help Winnie, the guest messaging assistant, and Winston, the guest tablet assistant, handle requests for accommodation invoices.

What it does

For a confirmed reservation, the workflow uses reservation and payment information supplied by the booking management system to prepare a PDF invoice, save it in the host’s Google Drive, and, when delivery is enabled, schedule a download link after checkout. Host review and delivery controls apply.

Google Drive connection

Only the host authorizes the Google Drive connection. Guests do not sign in to Google through this application. The connection supports creating invoice folders, uploading PDFs, and managing permissions for individual invoice links.

This website provides information about the application. It contains no guest invoices or booking records.
