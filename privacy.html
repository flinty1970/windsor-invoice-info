Privacy policy

This policy describes the intended data practices of Windsor Guest Invoice Assistant, operated by Martin Flint. The invoice workflow is currently being set up.

Information processed

The workflow processes information needed to prepare accommodation invoices: guest names, booking references, accommodation and stay dates, reservation charges, currency, taxes where recorded, and payment information. Additional billing details are included only when supplied and needed. Invoice requests may be received through Winnie or Winston.

Google user data

The host’s Google authorization is used to create or locate invoice folders, upload PDF invoices, and set or inspect sharing permissions. Folder and file identifiers and invoice links may be recorded for delivery and to avoid duplicates. OAuth credentials are stored privately on the host’s server, outside the public source repository. The invoice workflow is intended to operate in the designated guest invoice folder rather than browse unrelated Drive content.

Use and sharing

Information is used to prepare, store and deliver invoices, manage requests, and diagnose errors. Google Drive stores the PDFs; Hospitable carries guest messages and invoice links. Assistant infrastructure may process invoice requests to identify the requested task. Invoice links may be configured so anyone possessing the link can view the PDF; recipients should keep them private. The entire invoice folder is not intended to be publicly shared.

Google user data accessed through this invoice integration is not sold, used for advertising, or used by the operator to train generalized AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and controls

Invoices may be retained for accounting and record keeping obligations. Operational records are retained as needed for reliable delivery and troubleshooting. The host can disable automatic delivery and revoke Google authorization through Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete existing invoices.

Requests and contact

For questions, corrections, access requests or deletion requests, contact flinty@gmail.com. Requests are reviewed subject to applicable record keeping obligations.

This website

This static information site has no sign-in, forms, advertising or analytics added by the operator. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub’s own privacy statement.
