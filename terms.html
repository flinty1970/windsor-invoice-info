Terms of use

Windsor Guest Invoice Assistant is operated by Martin Flint to support accommodation invoice requests for his property. It is currently being set up and is not a public service for connecting other people’s Google accounts.

Invoice requests

Guests may request an invoice for their own reservation through the host’s supported guest communication channels. Requests before booking can receive confirmation that an invoice can be provided; an invoice requires a verified reservation and sufficient financial information.

Accuracy and delivery

Invoices are based on available reservation and payment records. Contact the host if details need correcting or if you require company billing details. Delivery time depends on the reservation’s checkout date, property timezone, delivery settings, successful preparation and third-party service availability. A scheduled delivery is confirmed only after it has been successfully recorded.

Host controls

The host controls the Google Drive connection and automatic delivery settings. Review or shadow modes may prepare an invoice without automatically sending it. The host can instead send the link manually.

Invoice links

Use invoice links only for the reservation to which they relate. Keep links private and do not access or distribute another guest’s invoice. This information website does not publish invoices.

Service limitations

The assistant supports administrative tasks and does not provide accounting, legal or tax advice. Booking terms and applicable rights remain governed by the reservation agreement and applicable law. These terms do not exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
